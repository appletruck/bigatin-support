Bigatin support

Get help with episodes, purchases, and Game Center.

Restore your episodes

Episodes 1–3 are free. The one-time Full Episodes Pack unlocks Episodes 4–6.

  1. Use the Apple Account that purchased the pack and connect to the internet.
  2. Open Bigatin and tap Lumaban na. In the episode list, tap the settings gear.
  3. Under Store, tap Restore Purchases. You can also restore from the Full Episodes Pack screen.

Apple can help with finding and restoring in-app purchases and billing questions.

Use Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone or iPad settings. Then open Bigatin and tap Game Center Leaderboard from the title screen.

Bigatin can send your combined score to the leaderboard while you’re signed in. Sign out of Game Center in device settings to play without submitting scores.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, the Bigatin version shown under Settings → About, and the steps that led to the issue. Keep account details, receipts, and passwords out of public reports.
