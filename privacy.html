Bigatin privacy policy

How the game and this support website handle information.

Game data on your device

Bigatin saves episode completion, high scores, and your subtitle, sound, and haptic preferences on your iPhone or iPad. These settings let the app remember your progress and choices. Bigatin operates without developer-run accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app, subject to any device backups you keep. Purchase history and any scores already submitted to Game Center are managed by Apple.

App Store purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. Bigatin reads verified purchase information from StoreKit to unlock or restore the Full Episodes Pack. Your payment details stay with Apple and its payment providers.

See Apple’s App Store privacy notice for how Apple handles purchase information.

Game Center scores

Game Center is optional. While you’re signed in, Bigatin can submit your combined score to Apple’s Game Center leaderboard. Other players may see your score, Game Center nickname, and avatar on the leaderboard.

You can sign out of Game Center in device settings to stop future score submissions. Apple manages Game Center records and privacy controls. See Apple’s Game Center privacy notice.

Support website and messages

These pages use no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

If you contact support, we use what you send to respond and retain relevant information while resolving or following up on the issue. Information you post in a public issue can be visible to others.

Privacy questions

Use Bigatin support to ask about this policy or request deletion of information you sent to support. Apple handles requests about your Apple Account, purchases, or Game Center records.
